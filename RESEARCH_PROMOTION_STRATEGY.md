## Phase 6: Call to Action - Policy Reform Petition

### 6.1 Primary Call to Action: Petition for Policy Reform

**Objective:**
Launch a petition urging the United Nations (UN), United States government, and World Bank to reassess and modify their policies on financing large families in low-income countries. The petition should highlight the correlation between high fertility rates and increased probabilities of conflict, emphasizing that current policies may inadvertently contribute to global instability.

### 6.2 Petition Content Framework

**Title:** "Stop Financing Population Growth in Low-Income Countries: A Path to Global Peace"

**Key Message:**
Research shows that countries with fertility rates above 5 have a 75% probability of war, while countries with fertility rates below 2 have only a 5-8% probability of war. Current international policies that finance large families in low-income countries may be contributing to global conflict. We call on the UN, US, and World Bank to redirect funding toward family planning and education instead of incentivizing high birth rates.

**Petition Demands:**
1. **United Nations:** Redirect UNFPA and related funding from programs that incentivize large families to comprehensive family planning and education initiatives
2. **United States:** Reform USAID and foreign assistance policies to prioritize family planning over child-focused aid that encourages population growth
3. **World Bank:** Modify International Development Association (IDA) and Global Financing Facility (GFF) policies to condition funding on family planning access and education

### 6.3 Relevant Policies and Links

#### United Nations Policies

**UNFPA (United Nations Population Fund)**
- **Website:** https://www.unfpa.org/
- **Family Planning Programs:** https://www.unfpa.org/family-planning
- **Current Funding Model:** UNFPA currently provides reproductive health services but funding may inadvertently support population growth in areas where family planning is limited
- **Policy Document:** UNFPA Strategic Plan 2022-2025: https://www.unfpa.org/strategic-plan
- **7 Billion Actions Campaign:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7_Billion_Actions

- **SDG Website:** https://sdgs.un.org/goals

**UN Population Division**
- **Website:** https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/

#### United States Policies

**USAID (United States Agency for International Development)**
- **Website:** https://www.usaid.gov/
- **Family Planning Programs:** https://www.usaid.gov/global-health/health-areas/family-planning
- **Current Policy:** USAID provides family planning services but also funds child-focused programs that may incentivize large families

**Helms Amendment (1973)**
- **Policy:** Restricts US foreign assistance funds from being used for abortion services
- **Impact:** May limit comprehensive family planning services
- **Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helms_Amendment_to_the_Foreign_Assistance_Act
- **Full Text:** Section 104(f) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961

**Mexico City Policy (Global Gag Rule)**
- **Policy:** Restricts US federal funding to NGOs that provide abortion counseling or services
- **Impact:** Affects comprehensive reproductive health services in low-income countries
- **Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City_policy
- **Current Status:** Policy has been reinstated and rescinded by different administrations

**State Department - Office of Population Affairs**
- **Website:** https://www.state.gov/bureaus-offices/under-secretary-for-economic-growth-energy-and-the-environment/bureau-of-population-refugees-and-migration/

## Research Topics Summary

### Topic 1: War and Economics

**Key Finding:**
- Countries with fertility rates > 5: 75% probability of war
- Countries with fertility rates < 2: 5-8% probability of war

**The Two Equilibria:**
1. **High Fertility Equilibrium:**
   - High fertility rates (> 5)
   - Young population
   - High risk of conflict (75%)

2. **Low Fertility Equilibrium:**
   - Low fertility rates (< 2)
   - Aging population
   - Low conflict probability (5-8%)

**Related Papers:**
- "The Vicious Cycle between Demography and War"
- "Impact of aging population on welfare and war"

### Topic 2: Geopolitics and Economic Policy

**Based on Two Papers:**

**Paper 1: Geopolitics and Trade Policy**
- The impact of national sovereignty
- Social identity factors
- Trade policy in multipolar world

**Paper 2: Geopolitics and Financial Contagion**
- Financial contagion in geopolitical contexts
- Balance of power periods
- Economic policy implications

**Related Papers:**
